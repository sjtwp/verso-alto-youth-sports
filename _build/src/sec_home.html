Who we are

Faith. Community. Sports.

A Christ-centered youth sports program built to help foster growth in faith, build community, and strive for greatness.

Faith

Christ-centered sports that help kids grow in body, mind, and spirit — on the field and off it.

Community

Families cheering side by side. Friendships that start on Saturday mornings and last for years.

Sports

Fun, affordable, skill-building play with no tryouts. Every kid gets in the game and strives for greatness.

The meaning behind the name
Verso l'alto means "to the heights" — the call of St. Pier Giorgio Frassati to strive for something greater.
