What to expect

Teamwork on every play

Our flag football program will teach the fundamentals — throwing, catching, running routes, pulling flags, and playing as a team — in a fun, non-contact setting where every player gets in the game.

  • Non-contact play — flags instead of tackling
  • Skill-building for beginners and growing players
  • Teamwork, discipline, and good sportsmanship
  • The same affordable, faith-centered approach as all our sports
