Our mission

Body, mind & spirit

Our mission is to provide families with affordable, Christ-centered youth sports — developing young athletes in body, mind, and spirit while strengthening families and communities.

We pursue excellence in sport, faith, character, and service. Children and families of all faiths are welcome.

Our name
Verso l'Alto

"To the heights"

Our name draws on St. Pier Giorgio Frassati's call to strive for something greater — in sport and in life.
