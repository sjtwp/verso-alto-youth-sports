Verso Alto Youth Sports, Inc.

Applies To: All participants, parents/guardians, coaches, volunteers, and athletic programs operated by Verso Alto Youth Sports, Inc.

1. Purpose

Verso Alto Youth Sports, Inc. (“Verso Alto Youth Sports”) is committed to providing a safe, positive, and faith-centered athletic environment for all children.

The purpose of this policy is to promote concussion awareness, establish appropriate procedures for responding to suspected head injuries, and prioritize the health and well-being of every participant.

2. Understanding Concussions

A concussion is a traumatic brain injury caused by a bump, blow, or jolt to the head or body that causes the brain to move rapidly within the skull.

Concussions may occur without a loss of consciousness, and symptoms may not appear immediately.

Possible signs and symptoms include:

  • Headache or pressure in the head
  • Dizziness, balance difficulties, or unusual clumsiness
  • Confusion, memory problems, or difficulty concentrating
  • Nausea or vomiting
  • Blurred vision or sensitivity to light or noise
  • Unusual fatigue, irritability, or changes in behavior
  • Appearing dazed, stunned, or unusually quiet

For younger children who may have difficulty describing symptoms, coaches and parents should watch for unusual crying, changes in behavior, loss of interest in activities, or difficulty walking or maintaining balance.

3. Immediate Removal From Play

Any participant who exhibits signs or symptoms of a concussion, or who is suspected of sustaining a concussion, must be immediately removed from all athletic activities.

The participant may not return to practice, competition, or other athletic activities on the same day, even if symptoms appear to resolve.

Coaches and volunteers must prioritize player safety over athletic participation.

When in doubt, the participant will be removed from play.

4. Parent/Guardian Notification

When a concussion is suspected, a coach or authorized league representative will:

  • Immediately remove the participant from athletic activity.
  • Notify the participant's parent or legal guardian as soon as reasonably possible.
  • Recommend prompt evaluation by an appropriate licensed healthcare professional.
  • Document the incident and notify Verso Alto Youth Sports leadership.

A participant with a suspected concussion must not be left unattended while awaiting appropriate care or transfer to a parent/guardian or medical professional.

5. Emergency Medical Situations

Emergency medical services (911) must be contacted when a participant experiences potentially serious symptoms, including:

  • Loss of consciousness or difficulty waking
  • Seizures or convulsions
  • Repeated vomiting
  • Increasing confusion, agitation, or unusual behavior
  • A worsening or severe headache
  • Weakness, numbness, or difficulty speaking
  • Unequal pupils or double vision
  • Significant difficulty walking or maintaining balance

Coaches and volunteers should not attempt to move a participant when a serious neck or spinal injury is suspected, except when necessary to prevent immediate danger.

6. Medical Evaluation and Return to Play

Any participant removed from activity because of a suspected concussion must receive an appropriate medical evaluation before returning to athletic participation.

A participant may return to athletic activity only when:

  • The participant has been evaluated by an appropriate licensed healthcare professional.
  • Written medical clearance authorizing a return to athletic participation has been provided to Verso Alto Youth Sports.
  • The participant has completed any recommended recovery or graduated return-to-play process.
  • The parent or legal guardian has been informed of and supports the participant's return.

A parent or guardian's permission alone does not replace required medical clearance.

Verso Alto Youth Sports reserves the right to delay a participant's return if additional safety concerns remain.

7. Coach and Volunteer Responsibilities

Coaches and volunteers are expected to:

  • Maintain awareness of concussion signs and symptoms.
  • Promptly remove participants with suspected concussions.
  • Notify parents/guardians and league leadership of suspected injuries.
  • Follow established emergency procedures.
  • Never pressure or encourage an injured participant to continue playing.
  • Follow applicable league safety requirements and age-appropriate rules.

Coaches and volunteers are not expected to diagnose concussions or provide medical treatment beyond their training.

8. Parent/Guardian Responsibilities

Parents and legal guardians are expected to:

  • Inform coaches of known injuries, concussions, or relevant medical restrictions.
  • Monitor their children for symptoms following a suspected head injury.
  • Seek appropriate medical evaluation when a concussion is suspected.
  • Follow medical recommendations regarding recovery and activity restrictions.
  • Provide required written medical clearance before their child returns to play.

9. Age-Appropriate Safety Measures

Verso Alto Youth Sports recognizes that younger children may be particularly vulnerable to injury and may have difficulty communicating symptoms.

The organization will promote age-appropriate instruction, safe playing environments, appropriate supervision, and rules designed to reduce unnecessary head contact.

For youth soccer, deliberate heading will not be taught or permitted for participants age 10 and younger.

Coaches should emphasize safe play, proper technique, and good sportsmanship.

10. Incident Documentation

Suspected concussions and significant head injuries must be reported to Verso Alto Youth Sports leadership and documented in an incident report.

Incident documentation should include the date, location, circumstances of the injury, observed symptoms, actions taken, and parent/guardian notification.

Medical information and incident records will be handled confidentially and shared only as reasonably necessary for participant safety, administration, insurance, or legal compliance.

11. Policy Compliance

All coaches, volunteers, parents, and participants are expected to cooperate with this policy.

Verso Alto may restrict participation when necessary to protect the health and safety of a participant.

The organization may update this policy to reflect changes in applicable laws, medical guidance, insurance requirements, or league operations.
