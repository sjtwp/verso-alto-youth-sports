Soccer

A six-week, co-ed recreational soccer program for kids ages 3 to 8. No tryouts — just great soccer, great families.

● Our first season · Registration coming soon
Co-ed soccer

More than a game

Kids build real soccer skills while they grow in faith and character.

  • Six-week recreational season — Saturday mornings anticipated
  • Co-ed teams for ages 3–4, 5–6, and 7–8
  • No tryouts — every child plays
  • Fields in the East Valley (location to be announced)
  • Arizona ESA funds accepted
