Choosing Soccer Cleats and Shin Guards for Young Kids

SoccerNovember 10, 2026· 2 min read· By Verso Alto Youth Sports

You don't need expensive gear for youth soccer. Here's what actually matters for players ages 3–8.

Shin guards (required)

  • Choose a size based on your child's height — check the manufacturer's chart.
  • Slip-in guards with ankle protection are easy for little ones.
  • Guards should cover most of the shin, from just below the knee to above the ankle.

Cleats or athletic shoes

  • For the youngest players, comfortable athletic shoes work fine.
  • If you buy cleats, choose molded rubber cleats designed for grass.
  • Leave about a thumb's width of room at the toe.
  • Velcro straps are great for kids who can't tie laces yet.

Socks

Tall soccer socks hold shin guards in place and protect skin from turf and sun.

Save money

Kids grow fast! Hand-me-downs, swaps with other families, and secondhand sports stores in the East Valley are great options.
