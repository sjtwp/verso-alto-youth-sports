Building Character Through Youth Sports: The Virtues We Teach on the Field

FaithSeptember 1, 2026· 2 min read· By Verso Alto Youth Sports

Skills matter. But years from now, most kids won't remember the score of a U6 soccer game. They will remember how sports made them feel — and who they became along the way. At Verso Alto, character comes first.

Patience

Learning a new skill takes time. Missing a kick, trying again, and improving bit by bit teaches kids that growth is a process.

Courage

Stepping onto the field, trying something new, or playing a new position takes courage. We celebrate effort, not perfection.

Humility

Winning with grace and losing with dignity are lifelong lessons. Our players shake hands, thank officials, and cheer for opponents' good plays.

Charity and teamwork

Passing the ball, helping a fallen teammate, and including the new kid are small acts of love. Teamwork is charity in action.

Perseverance

Arizona heat, early mornings, tough games — sports give kids a safe place to practice not giving up.

How we teach virtue

  • Practices and games include prayer and an emphasis on sportsmanship.
  • Families are encouraged to talk about a virtue at home each week.
  • We recognize sportsmanship and effort, not just goals.

These ideas come straight from our name — read what "Verso l'Alto" means. You can also explore how to carry these lessons home after the game.

Want to help teach these virtues? Consider coaching or support our mission.
