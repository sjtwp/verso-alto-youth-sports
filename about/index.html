About us

Faith. Family.
Greatness.

Verso Alto Youth Sports gives Catholic families affordable, Christ-centered youth sports.

Our mission

Body, mind & spirit

Our mission is to provide Catholic families with affordable, Christ-centered youth sports — developing young athletes in body, mind, and spirit while strengthening families and communities.

We pursue excellence in sport, faith, character, and service. Children and families of all faiths are welcome.

Based in Gilbert and serving families across Arizona's East Valley — including Mesa, Chandler, Queen Creek and Tempe — we're starting with co-ed youth soccer and growing into an all-sport league. Read our story on the blog.

Our name
Verso l'Alto

"To the heights"

Our name draws on St. Pier Giorgio Frassati's call to strive for something greater — in sport and in life.
