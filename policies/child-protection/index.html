Child Protection & Safe Environment Policy

← All policies & forms

Verso Alto Youth Sports, Inc.

Applies To: All directors, officers, coaches, assistant coaches, volunteers, employees, contractors, and other individuals serving in roles involving contact with minors through Verso Alto Youth Sports, Inc.

1. Purpose

Verso Alto Youth Sports, Inc. (“Verso Alto Youth Sports”) is committed to providing a safe, welcoming, and faith-centered environment where children can grow in character, friendship, sportsmanship, and love of God.

The safety, dignity, and well-being of every child entrusted to our programs are of the highest importance.

This policy establishes standards and procedures intended to prevent abuse, neglect, misconduct, and inappropriate behavior while promoting accountability among all individuals involved in Verso Alto Youth Sports activities.

Verso Alto Youth Sports maintains a zero-tolerance policy toward child abuse, exploitation, harassment, and intentional misconduct involving minors.

2. Scope and Responsibilities

This policy applies to all Verso Alto Youth Sports-sponsored practices, games, events, meetings, and other activities involving minors.

All covered individuals are responsible for:

  • Protecting the physical and emotional well-being of participants.
  • Maintaining appropriate boundaries with minors.
  • Following established supervision and safety procedures.
  • Promptly reporting suspected abuse, neglect, or misconduct.
  • Cooperating with investigations and corrective actions.
  • Treating all participants and families with dignity and respect.

3. Background Checks and Volunteer Screening

All adult coaches, assistant coaches, and other adults serving in positions involving regular interaction with or responsibility for minors must complete an approved criminal background screening before beginning their duties.

Verso Alto Youth Sports may require background checks for additional positions based on the nature of the individual's responsibilities.

Screening requirements include:

  • Completion of a volunteer application or registration.
  • Disclosure of relevant criminal history or disqualifying circumstances.
  • Successful completion of an organization-approved background check.
  • Agreement to follow Verso Alto Youth Sports policies and standards of conduct.

Background checks must be renewed at least every two years, or more frequently if required by law, insurance, or an applicable governing organization.

Verso Alto Youth Sports reserves the right to deny, suspend, or terminate volunteer participation based on screening results or other credible safety concerns.

A background check does not replace ongoing supervision or adherence to this policy.

4. Safe Environment Training

Verso Alto Youth Sports may require all adult coaches and volunteers who supervise or regularly interact with minors to complete child protection and safe environment training approved by Verso Alto Youth Sports before assuming their responsibilities.

Training should address:

  • Recognizing signs of child abuse and neglect.
  • Appropriate adult-child boundaries.
  • Safe supervision practices.
  • Responding to suspected abuse or misconduct.
  • Reporting responsibilities.
  • Appropriate communication with minors and families.

Verso Alto Youth Sports may recognize equivalent training completed through a Catholic diocese, parish, school, or other approved youth-serving organization, provided it meets the organization's requirements.

Training must be renewed as determined by Verso Alto Youth Sports and any applicable governing requirements.

5. Adult Supervision and the Two-Adult Standard

Verso Alto Youth Sports will make reasonable efforts to ensure that at least two screened and approved adults are present during organized activities involving minors.

Adults must avoid isolated, one-on-one situations with unrelated minors.

When two approved adults are not available:

  • Activities should remain in open, observable areas.
  • Parents or guardians should remain nearby or be asked to assist with supervision.
  • Coaches must avoid private or secluded interactions with children.
  • The activity may be modified, delayed, or canceled when adequate supervision cannot be maintained.

An adult must never arrange a private, isolated meeting with an unrelated minor through a Verso Alto program.

6. Appropriate Conduct and Physical Contact

Coaches and volunteers must model Christian values, good sportsmanship, patience, and respect.

Appropriate conduct includes:

  • Using encouraging and age-appropriate language.
  • Treating children fairly and respectfully.
  • Providing instruction without humiliation or intimidation.
  • Maintaining professional and appropriate physical boundaries.
  • Using physical contact only when reasonably necessary for safety, instruction, or age-appropriate encouragement.

Prohibited conduct includes:

  • Physical, sexual, or emotional abuse.
  • Corporal punishment or physical discipline.
  • Bullying, hazing, harassment, threats, or intimidation.
  • Sexualized language, jokes, gestures, or inappropriate conversations.
  • Unnecessary or inappropriate touching.
  • Favoritism intended to create secrecy, dependence, or inappropriate relationships.
  • Possession or use of alcohol, illegal drugs, or impairing substances while supervising children.
  • Any behavior that places a participant at unreasonable risk.

7. Restrooms, Changing Areas, and Personal Assistance

Coaches and volunteers should encourage children to use restroom facilities with assistance from their own parent or legal guardian whenever practical.

Adults must not enter a restroom or changing area alone with an unrelated child except when an immediate safety concern requires intervention.

When assistance is necessary:

  • A parent or guardian should be contacted whenever practical.
  • Another approved adult should be present or within sight or hearing.
  • The child's privacy and dignity must be respected.
  • Assistance should be limited to what is reasonably necessary.

Verso Alto Youth Sports does not permit adults to photograph or record children in restrooms or changing areas.

8. Transportation and Release of Participants

Parents and legal guardians are generally responsible for transporting their children to and from Verso Alto Youth Sports activities.

Coaches and volunteers should not transport unrelated minors individually in personal vehicles as part of league activities without prior authorization from Verso Alto Youth Sports leadership and the child's parent or guardian.

Children must be released only to their parent, legal guardian, or another individual authorized by the parent or guardian.

Coaches must not knowingly leave a child unattended following an organized activity.

9. Communication With Minors

Communication regarding Verso Alto Youth Sports activities should ordinarily occur directly with parents or legal guardians.

Coaches and volunteers should use organization-approved communication channels whenever available.

Private electronic communication between an adult volunteer and an unrelated minor is prohibited unless necessary for an immediate safety concern.

When communication with a minor is necessary, a parent or guardian or another approved adult should be included.

Coaches and volunteers must not establish inappropriate private relationships with participants through social media, text messaging, or other communication platforms.

10. Photography, Video, and Participant Privacy

Photography and video recording may occur during Verso Alto Youth Sports activities in accordance with the organization's registration agreements, media acknowledgments, and privacy practices.

Coaches and volunteers must:

  • Respect applicable parental restrictions and organizational media policies.
  • Avoid photographing children in private or sensitive situations.
  • Refrain from sharing identifying or sensitive participant information without authorization.
  • Use photographs and videos only for appropriate organizational purposes.

11. Recognizing and Reporting Suspected Abuse

All individuals covered by this policy must take suspected child abuse, neglect, exploitation, or serious misconduct seriously.

When abuse or neglect is suspected:

  • Ensure immediate safety. If a child is in immediate danger, call 911.
  • Report to the appropriate authorities. Any individual who is required to report under Arizona law must promptly make a report to law enforcement or the Arizona Department of Child Safety, as applicable. Verso Alto Youth Sports requires its personnel to promptly report reasonable suspicions of child abuse to the appropriate authorities, even if their individual legal reporting status is uncertain.
  • Notify Verso Alto Youth Sports leadership. Inform the President/Executive Director or another uninvolved board officer as soon as reasonably possible, without delaying a required report to authorities.
  • Preserve relevant information. Document observations, statements, dates, and circumstances accurately.
  • Avoid independent investigations. Do not interrogate the child, confront the accused, or attempt to determine whether an allegation is true.

An internal report to Verso Alto Youth Sports does not replace any legally required report to public authorities.

Individuals should not promise absolute confidentiality to a child making a disclosure.

12. Response to Allegations and Misconduct

When Verso Alto Youth Sports receives a credible allegation or reasonable suspicion of abuse, exploitation, or serious misconduct involving a covered individual, the organization will take appropriate protective measures.

These may include:

  • Immediately restricting or suspending the individual's involvement with minors while concerns are assessed.
  • Cooperating with law enforcement and child protective authorities.
  • Preserving relevant records and communications.
  • Notifying the Board of Directors as appropriate.
  • Taking disciplinary or administrative action consistent with applicable law and organizational policy.

Verso Alto Youth Sports will not retaliate against any person who makes a good-faith report of suspected abuse or misconduct.

13. Parent and Guardian Responsibilities

Parents and legal guardians are important partners in maintaining a safe environment.

Parents are encouraged to:

  • Review league safety policies.
  • Communicate relevant safety or supervision concerns.
  • Ensure appropriate drop-off and pickup arrangements.
  • Report suspected misconduct promptly.
  • Reinforce respectful behavior and appropriate boundaries with their children.

Parents are welcome to observe ordinary practices and games, subject to reasonable field access and safety rules.

14. Policy Enforcement

Violations of this policy may result in corrective action, suspension, removal from volunteer responsibilities, termination of involvement, or referral to appropriate authorities.

Verso Alto Youth Sports reserves the right to take immediate protective action when necessary to safeguard participants.

15. Policy Review and Updates

The Board of Directors will review this policy at least annually and may update it as necessary to reflect changes in applicable laws, insurance requirements, child protection guidance, and organizational operations.

All covered individuals are expected to comply with the most current approved version.
