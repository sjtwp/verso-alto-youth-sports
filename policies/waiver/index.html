Parent/Guardian Participation Agreement, Waiver & Release of Liability

← All policies & forms

Verso Alto Youth Sports, Inc. · Version 1.0 · Effective November 13th, 2026

By registering my child or children to participate in programs offered by Verso Alto Youth Sports, Inc. (“Verso Alto Youth Sports”), I acknowledge and agree to the following terms on behalf of myself and, to the extent permitted by applicable law, my participating child or children.

1. Voluntary Participation and Assumption of Risk

I understand that participation in youth sports and recreational activities involves inherent risks, including but not limited to falls, collisions, physical contact, equipment-related injuries, weather conditions, heat-related illness, and other risks that may result in injury.

I voluntarily authorize my child to participate in Verso Alto Youth Sports programs, including practices, games, training sessions, and related league activities. I acknowledge these risks and understand that Verso Alto Youth Sports cannot guarantee an injury-free environment.

2. Medical Authorization and Emergency Care

I certify that my child is physically able to participate, subject to any medical conditions, limitations, or accommodations I have disclosed to Verso Alto Youth Sports.

In the event of an injury or medical emergency, I authorize Verso Alto Youth Sports' coaches, volunteers, and representatives to seek appropriate emergency medical assistance for my child if I cannot be reached promptly.

I understand that Verso Alto Youth Sports does not guarantee the availability of medical personnel during activities and that I am responsible for medical expenses incurred on behalf of my child, except as otherwise required by law.

I agree to provide accurate emergency contact information and notify Verso Alto Youth Sports of relevant medical restrictions or accommodations.

3. Release of Liability

To the fullest extent permitted by Arizona law, I release and agree not to hold liable Verso Alto Youth Sports, Inc., its directors, officers, employees, coaches, volunteers, representatives, and participating facilities for claims arising from ordinary negligence in connection with participation in league activities.

This release applies to claims belonging to me as the parent or legal guardian. Any purported release of claims belonging independently to my minor child applies only to the extent enforceable under applicable law.

Nothing in this agreement is intended to release liability for gross negligence, reckless conduct, intentional misconduct, or any liability that cannot legally be waived.

4. Parent/Guardian Responsibilities and Code of Conduct

I agree to:

  • Encourage good sportsmanship, teamwork, respect, and positive behavior.
  • Treat coaches, officials, volunteers, participants, and other families respectfully.
  • Follow league rules, safety procedures, and instructions.
  • Ensure my child is appropriately supervised before and after scheduled activities.
  • Promptly communicate safety concerns, injuries, or relevant changes in my child's participation needs.
  • Refrain from abusive, threatening, discriminatory, or disruptive behavior.

I understand that Verso Alto Youth Sports may take reasonable disciplinary action, including suspension or removal from activities, for serious or repeated violations of league policies.

5. Faith-Based Mission

I understand that Verso Alto Youth Sports is a Catholic youth sports organization committed to fostering faith, family, sportsmanship, teamwork, and character development.

League activities may include prayer, Christian teachings, and age-appropriate expressions of Catholic values.

Participation is open to children and families of all faith backgrounds, and being Catholic is not a requirement for participation.

6. Snacks, Food Allergies, and Dietary Restrictions

I understand that snacks, beverages, or other food items may be provided or shared by parents, guardians, coaches, volunteers, or other participants during league activities.

I agree to disclose any known food allergies, dietary restrictions, or related medical conditions affecting my child during registration and to promptly notify Verso Alto Youth Sports of any changes.

I understand that:

  • Verso Alto Youth Sports will make reasonable efforts to communicate known food allergies and relevant safety precautions to appropriate coaches and volunteers.
  • Parents and guardians are responsible for ensuring that food and beverages consumed by their children are appropriate for their individual dietary and medical needs.
  • Food items may contain allergens or come into contact with allergens during preparation, packaging, or distribution.
  • Verso Alto Youth Sports cannot guarantee an allergen-free environment.
  • Parents and guardians of children with severe allergies are responsible for communicating necessary precautions and providing prescribed emergency medication, together with appropriate instructions and arrangements for its availability and administration.

I understand that allergic reactions and other food-related incidents may occur despite reasonable precautions. In the event of a suspected serious allergic reaction or medical emergency, Verso Alto Youth Sports representatives may seek emergency medical assistance in accordance with Section 2 of this agreement.

7. League Policies and Program Changes

I understand that Verso Alto Youth Sports may modify schedules, locations, team assignments, program activities, or other arrangements due to weather, facility availability, enrollment, safety considerations, or circumstances beyond its control.

I agree to follow applicable league policies and understand that refunds, cancellations, and credits are governed by the organization's separately communicated refund policy.

8. Photography and Video

I understand that permission to photograph or record my child is requested separately during registration.

My decision to grant or decline photo and video consent does not affect my child's eligibility to participate.

9. Acknowledgment and Electronic Consent

By electronically acknowledging and signing the accompanying registration form, I confirm that:

  • I am the parent or legal guardian of the child or children being registered, or otherwise have legal authority to provide consent.
  • I have read and understand this agreement in its entirety.
  • I understand that participation in youth sports involves inherent risks.
  • I voluntarily authorize my child or children to participate in Verso Alto Youth Sports programs.
  • I understand that this agreement includes a release of certain legal claims.
  • I agree to the terms outlined above.

This agreement applies to all children identified in the accompanying registration submission. My electronic acknowledgment and signature on that submission constitute my acceptance of this agreement.

Version 1.0 | Effective November 13th, 2026
