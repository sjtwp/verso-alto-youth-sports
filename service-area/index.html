Service area

Serving Arizona

Our first season is played in the East Valley — and families from all 91 cities and towns in Arizona are welcome to join our community, follow our blog, and use eligible Arizona ESA funds.

Where we play

East Valley home field

Spring 2027 co-ed youth soccer will be played on Saturday mornings at fields in the East Valley (location to be announced). Gilbert, Mesa, Chandler, Queen Creek and Tempe families are just minutes away.

Don't see games near you yet? As we grow into baseball, basketball, and football, we hope to reach more Arizona communities. Join the interest list to tell us where you are.

East Valley — our home field

Maricopa & Pinal Counties · 9 cities & towns

Gilbert, AZ

Our home base. Gilbert's family-centered neighborhoods, parks like Freestone and Discovery, and strong parish communities make it the heart of Verso Alto Youth Sports and the focus of our first Spring 2027 soccer season.

Mesa, AZ

Arizona's third-largest city and one of the most family-dense communities in the Valley. Mesa families are minutes from our planned East Valley fields and are a big part of the community we're building.

Chandler, AZ

Home to thriving Catholic parishes and schools, Chandler is a natural fit for faith-centered youth sports. Families from Chandler and South Chandler are a short drive from our East Valley programs.

Queen Creek, AZ

One of the fastest-growing towns in Arizona, Queen Creek is full of young families looking for affordable, values-driven activities. Our Saturday-morning soccer is built for exactly that.

Tempe, AZ

Tempe families bring energy and community spirit. Whether you're near Kiwanis Park or south Tempe, our East Valley programs are an easy weekend drive.

Apache Junction, AZ

Sitting at the foot of the Superstition Mountains, Apache Junction families are welcome to join our East Valley leagues — mountain views and all.

Fountain Hills, AZ

Known for its famous fountain and tight-knit community, Fountain Hills is a short drive from the East Valley and a great fit for families seeking faith-based youth sports.

Guadalupe, AZ

A small town with a rich cultural and faith heritage, Guadalupe sits right between Tempe and Phoenix — close to everything our East Valley programs offer.

Florence, AZ

The historic Pinal County seat and its growing neighborhoods toward the San Tan area are home to many young families who are a quick drive from our East Valley fields.
